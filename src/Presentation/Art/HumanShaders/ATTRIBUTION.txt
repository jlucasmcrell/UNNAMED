apply_human_skin.gd is the local texture adapter. It replaces only the
HumGen HG_Body_ material. Body albedo/normal/green-channel roughness and
scalar factors come from the imported material. Micro detail, procedural
noise, AO, and translucency are disabled unless supplied by a future test.
The original shader's wrapped diffuse, double specularity, and close-range
subsurface response remain enabled. Its original SSS depth gate drops to
zero beyond one meter; this test does not silently change that behavior.
