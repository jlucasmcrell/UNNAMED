# Siann Gate 1b

This revision follows the owner's 2026-10-27 11:10, 11:15 and 11:20 rulings, which supersede the earlier warm skin and age-dependent hair direction. It is a review candidate, not an accepted public replacement.

The face target is a petite surface/moon-elf family: a narrow tapering face, small chin, high cheeks, thin straight nose, moderate upswept almond eyes, small delicate mouth, slender neck and serene rest. Native HumGen catalogue thumbnails were inspected before choosing Debra, Clara, Jessica, Nico, Jude and Caucasian 4. There is no separately installed "beauty" preset collection. Native face controls refine these starting identities; the exact six recipes are in `F1_SIANN_GATE1B_RECIPE.json`.

Skin is chalk white with native normal/roughness detail and the existing subsurface shader. Original albedo chroma is suppressed so baked brown eye sockets cannot survive as a pigment tint. The pearl layer is weighted toward grazing angles; its subdued pink–green–blue path avoids violet. Hair keeps the native directional atlas and receives a small additive pearl specular band. The white palette applies at every age. Brows/cap and lashes are dyed independently of hair lengths. Facial hair is removed through HumGen on male exports and suppressed in the Siann runtime path, including LOD visibility.

The native export keeps face and age keys, bakes the already approved petite body proportions, separates haircap from skin, and uses 4K body / 1K eyes / 2K hair textures. Narrower cards and denser, smoother groom children replace the earlier broad-card settings. Motion is rebuilt against each exact exported target through the existing Epic pipeline; no rest-pose conversion or authored replacement motion is introduced.

Installed ear controls cover height, width, turn, lobe and antihelix shape. No pointed-tip preset/key was found. Native ear geometry is retained; a sculpted elf tip requires coordinator approval and is outside this revision.

Gate 1b proof: six noon closeups, six noon 5.5 m gameplay views, the matching dusk views and one noon/dusk pearl turntable. Full-resolution native frames and asset hashes accompany the review. The archived before has different historical framing; it is not a controlled lighting A/B. The turntable uses actual camera samples, with repeated presentation frames rather than generated interpolation.

Gate 2 creator dials and the other peoples remain deferred until Claude accepts the Siann faces. No public bindings, push or promotion are authorized by this gate.
