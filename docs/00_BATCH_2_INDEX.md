## Cross-system principles

1. The simulation knows truth; characters know only what they perceive, infer, remember, or are told.
2. The world continues when the player is absent.
3. NPCs use the same broad rules as the player wherever practical.
4. Failure should often transform content instead of merely deleting it.
5. Magic is systemic and compositional; religion and science are competing or overlapping interpretations of real phenomena.
6. The Soul is an in-world continuity entity above individual incarnations.
7. Large systems should ship in layers: prove a narrow mechanic, make it playable, integrate it systemically, then add long-horizon and cosmic consequences.
