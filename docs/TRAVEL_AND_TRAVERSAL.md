# OTHERREACH — Travel & Traversal

**Status:** Strong working direction  
**M2 impact:** None

## 1. Goal

Travel should progressively change from survival and navigation into mastery of the world's transportation networks without making geography meaningless.

Fast travel is **earned, discovered, constructed or learned**.

## 2. Travel tiers

### Tier 1 — Ordinary movement
- walking;
- sprinting;
- climbing;
- swimming;
- roads;
- ferries/boats.

### Tier 2 — Faster physical travel
- mounts;
- carts;
- wagons;
- ships;
- culture-specific vehicles.

### Tier 3 — Extraordinary physical movement
- gliding;
- Kal wing-assisted traversal;
- magical flight;
- flying mounts;
- specialized climbing/traversal equipment.

### Tier 4 — Stable point-to-point travel
- discovered Othergates;
- known Otherways;
- ritual travel;
- constructed transit infrastructure;
- recall anchors.

### Tier 5 — Space-time traversal
- unstable Otherfolds;
- Otherwhere;
- Otherwhen;
- deep Otherways;
- ancient gate networks;
- wormhole-like phenomena.

## 3. Otherways are places, not menu buttons

Where practical, a player physically approaches and enters a gate or route.

Routes may differ by:

- capacity;
- stability;
- destination knowledge;
- cargo limits;
- mount compatibility;
- companion compatibility;
- danger;
- cost;
- activation conditions.

## 4. Culture-specific travel

Different peoples may begin with different transportation traditions:

- mounts;
- gate geometry;
- druidic/root paths;
- mechanical transit;
- planar walking;
- advanced vehicles.

Cultural familiarity can grant early access without permanently locking outsiders out of learned travel systems.

True biological capabilities remain exceptions.

## 5. Cargo and logistics

Travel methods should matter to inventory/logistics:

- a horse can carry more than a person;
- a wagon requires roads;
- some Otherways cannot admit large cargo;
- banks/couriers may use established gate routes;
- caravans create economic risk and opportunity.

## 6. Flight

Flight should not trivialize world design.

Constraints may include:

- stamina;
- weather;
- altitude;
- carried mass;
- predators;
- restricted airspace;
- magical/technical cost;
- takeoff/landing requirements.

Kal wings are primarily a distinct biological traversal tool, not universal unrestricted flight in the present environment.

## 7. Discovery and mapping

Travel networks should be recorded as character knowledge.

Finding a gate does not necessarily mean knowing:

- destination;
- activation;
- safety;
- schedule;
- cost.

Cartographers, scholars and explorers can sell or share this information.

## 8. Streaming use

Natural travel transitions can conceal technical streaming boundaries:

- caves;
- tunnels;
- gates;
- ships;
- lifts;
- Otherways.

This is an implementation convenience, not a reason to fragment the world gratuitously.

## 9. Future multiplayer possibility

If community-hosted worlds ever federate, an Othergate is a natural fiction for server transfer.

That is explicitly **future design space**, not a current architecture requirement.
