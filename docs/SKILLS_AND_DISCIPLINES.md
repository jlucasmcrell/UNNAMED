# OTHERREACH — Skills, Disciplines & Knowledge

**Status:** Reconciled 2026-09-23 (`PROGRESSION_AXIS_RECONCILIATION.md`). Skills & disciplines are `AX-SKL`, including weapon families, magic domains and crafting; techniques are `AX-TEC`. `PROGRESSION.md` is normative.  
**M2 impact:** None

## 1. Direction

Otherreach should have broad, interconnected skills without reducing progression to repeated +1% bonuses.

The preferred structure is closer to **discipline webs** than rigid linear trees.

## 3. Skill vs technique

Skill measures general competence/reliability.

Techniques define specific capabilities:

Example within Polearms:
- reach control;
- bracing;
- mounted use;
- throwing;
- counterattacks;
- staff transition;
- magical channeling.

Two characters with equal numeric competence can know different techniques.

## 4. Cross-discipline techniques

Some advanced capabilities require combinations.

Examples:

**Polearms + Runecraft + Artificing → transforming recall-pike**

**Anatomy + Marksmanship → precise species-specific weak-point shot**

**Geology + Construction → superior foundation choice**

## 5. Cultural/scientific traditions

A civilization may start with knowledge unknown elsewhere.

Outsiders can generally learn cultural/scientific systems if they acquire:

- language;
- foundational concepts;
- teachers;
- tools;
- trust/access.

True innate biology remains nonlearnable except through extraordinary alteration.

## 6. Learning philosophy

Prefer learning through:

- meaningful use;
- study;
- teachers;
- experimentation;
- exploration;
- first successful execution;
- reverse engineering;
- difficult challenges.

Avoid grind that rewards thousands of trivial identical actions.

## 7. Current progression-contract tension

Existing `PROGRESSION.md` currently separates:

- character level;
- skills;
- weapon mastery;
- magic mastery;
- abilities/talents;
- professions;
- reputation;
- equipment;
- companions.

This document does not override those axes.

Post-M2 reconciliation must decide how “techniques,” “discipline webs,” and broad learnable cultural systems map onto existing AX-SKL / AX-WM / AX-MM / AX-ABL / AX-PRF without duplicating currencies.

**Resolved 2026-09-23.** `AX-WM`, `AX-MM` and `AX-PRF` merged into skills (competence) and techniques (capability); `AX-ABL` became the technique web, learned rather than bought. See `PROGRESSION_AXIS_RECONCILIATION.md`.

## 8. Race rule

> Race can change what comes naturally or what is biologically possible; it should not casually decide what knowledge a character may ever learn.

## 9. UI goal

Players should be able to understand:

- what they know;
- what they are good at;
- what prerequisite they lack;
- what teacher/knowledge/tool can bridge the gap.

Avoid opaque “wrong race/wrong class” messages when a concrete reason exists.
