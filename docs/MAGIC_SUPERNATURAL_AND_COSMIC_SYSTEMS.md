# OTHERREACH — Magic, Supernatural & Cosmic Systems

**Status:** Strong working design  
**M2 impact:** None

## Foundational idea

> **Magic is the ability to make a Pacha behave differently from the way it normally would.**

Science and magic are not opposites. Magic is real; science is one method of understanding real phenomena.

## Resonance and Strain

Otherreach does not require a universal mana pool.

### Resonance
A practitioner's ability to interface with Pacha phenomena.

### Strain
Accumulated stress from magical manipulation.

High strain can cause poor control, instability, physical symptoms, injury, unconsciousness, temporary resonance loss, or rare lasting consequences.

Characters also use ordinary Health, Stamina, and Focus.

Magic may additionally consume reagents, blood, charges, prepared runes, environmental conditions, ritual time, divine covenant, or technological energy.

## Vaskaal limitation

Vaskaal cannot directly cast because their biology lacks native Pacha resonance.

They can still measure, contain, store, disrupt, imitate, and engineer around magical phenomena.

## Domains vs traditions

A **domain** describes what is manipulated.

A **tradition** describes how a culture understands and performs it.

Different cultures may reach similar outcomes through different methods.

## Spell grammar

A custom spell can be assembled from:

- Domain;
- Intent;
- Target;
- Delivery;
- Shape;
- Magnitude;
- Duration;
- Behavior;
- Trigger;
- Anchor;
- Safeguards;
- Cost/strain profile.

Example:

**Thermal + Heat + Projectile + Sphere + Moderate + Impact Burst**

creates a fireball-like formula.

Change delivery, shape, duration, or anchor and the result changes.

## Player-created spells

Players learn magical primitives gradually.

They can:

- learn established formulas;
- tune formulas;
- save variants;
- invent combinations;
- name spells;
- teach them;
- inscribe them into items.

Custom design is optional depth, not mandatory busywork.

## Complexity and refinement

Complexity affects prerequisites, Focus, Strain, casting time, stability, and implement requirements.

Mastery includes efficiency: an expert may produce the same result with much less strain.

## Unsafe casting

If an effect is physically possible, the system should prefer clear risk over an arbitrary level lock.

Projected unsafe strain can cause collapse, injury, sensory damage, spell failure, Othermarking, or resonance loss.

## Magical scale

### Technique
Small, seconds.

### Spell
Individual combat/utility effect.

### Ritual
Prepared, minutes/hours, may use materials/participants.

### Working
Persistent enchantment, ward, anchor, or structure.

### Great Work
Civilizational/cosmic scale requiring infrastructure and specialists.

## Core domains

### Force / Evocation
Kinetic force, heat, cold, lightning, light, sound/concussion.

### Warding
Barriers, seals, anti-scrying, containment, alarms, reinforcement, Veil stabilization.

### Vital / Restoration
Bleeding control, tissue, bone, poison, disease, nerves, organs. Rapid healing can still impose recovery/nutritional cost.

### Necromancy / Soulcraft
Death-sense, spirit communication, corpse animation, soul binding, memory echoes, preservation, recall, resurrection research.

**Animated corpse is not automatically the returned person.**

### Transmutation
Reshape, harden, soften, reinforce, repair, alter physical properties.

### Conjuration / Summoning
Calling existing beings, opening passage, temporary constructs, binding/contracting.

### Illusion / Perception
False sensory information. A visual illusion does not automatically fool scent, touch, thermal sensing, or soul detection.

### Mind / Influence
Calm, fear, suggestion, emotional influence, memory interference, communication. Often highly regulated.

### Divination
Bounded information: traces, magical residue, possible futures, scrying, historical impressions. Results can have confidence rather than omniscience.

### Nature traditions
Plants, fungi, animals, weather, soil, ecosystems, natural traversal networks.

### Enchantment / Runecraft
Persistent magic using anchor, pattern, source, trigger, effect, and stability.

### Spatial / Othercraft
Distance, position, folds, anchors, Otherways, Otherwhere.

### Temporal / Otherwhen
Preservation, temporal echoes, limited process acceleration/deceleration; true Otherwhen travel is rare and major.

### Cosmic / Astromantic
Celestial bodies, gravity, stellar energy, Othertides, Pacha-scale resonance and large Veil phenomena.

Cosmic does not mean “bigger fireball.”

## Implements

Wands, staves, rods, scepters, orbs, grimoires, talismans, athames, runestones, censers, instruments, lenses, gauntlets, and body inscriptions can change precision, stability, throughput, preparation, or delivery.

A skilled caster may cast without an implement, but tools matter.

## No spell-slot limit

A practitioner may know many spells. Favorites/preparation affect speed and convenience, not whether the spell exists.

## Countermagic

Possible counters include interruption, warding, dispersion, grounding, counter-patterns, anchor disruption, focus destruction, and sensory denial.

## Magical evidence

Magic can leave residue, patterns, environmental effects, spirit disturbance, Otherwake, or casting signatures.

This feeds crime and investigation.

## Magic and armor

Avoid hard “mage cannot wear plate” rules.

Armor can affect movement, gesture, conductivity, resonance, heat, or weight. Characters can build around those constraints.

## Cross-cultural hybrid magic/technology

A single device might combine:

- Siann enchantment;
- Vaskaal control systems;
- Kal structural engineering;
- Ondrek material knowledge;
- Constructed formal patterning.

The melting-pot world should produce things no source civilization invented alone.

## Gods

Working canon:

> **Gods exist as beings or intelligences capable of actions mortals reasonably call divine. “God” describes relationship and scale, not necessarily the highest category of being.**

A god to mortals may not be a god to another god.

There may be no known top.

## Religious magic

Some supernatural effects may be invoked rather than personally cast.

A practitioner uses prayer, ritual, covenant, sacred geometry, offerings, liturgy, or community participation, and something else enables the effect.

This does not prove whether the mechanism is divine, technological, metaphysical, or mixed.

Gods can refuse or condition aid according to comprehensible values.

## Mor religion

Mor may personally communicate with a being they call their god.

They can know that being without understanding what it ultimately is.

## Constructed religion

Constructed objectively know their Pacha was built.

Their theology can ask whether the Authors were gods, who made the Authors, whether synthetic consciousness has a Soul, and whether editable reality resembles simulation.

## Vaskaal religion

Vaskaal may interpret magic as new physics, divine evidence, or both. Inability to cast does not imply lack of religion.

## Supernatural epistemology

> **Otherreach does not distinguish the supernatural by whether it is real. The phenomena are real; the uncertainty lies in what they ultimately are.**

Souls exist. God-like beings exist. Magic exists. The Other exists. Their deepest origins remain debatable.
