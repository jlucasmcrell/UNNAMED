# OTHERREACH — Souls, Death, Reincarnation & Legacy

**Status:** Foundational long-term design  
**M2 impact:** None  
**Architecture note:** This will eventually touch persistence/world/player identity and needs deliberate design before implementation.

## Core premise

A Soul is a real in-world continuity entity above an individual incarnation.

Conceptually:

**Soul → incarnation → body/mind/skills/equipment/history**

The player may have one persistent Soul in a world/save across multiple player characters.

## Body, Mind, Soul

### Body
Physical anatomy, senses, strength, motor adaptation, biological traits.

### Mind
Memory, cognition, personality, learned concepts.

### Soul
Persistent identity/continuity capable of surviving some forms of bodily death.

The Soul is not a perfect memory backup.

## World-bound Soul

Default:

A Soul belongs to one world/save.

A completely fresh world creates a new Soul. Future special modes may allow explicit Soul import.

## Soul Sheet

Character selection should expose a first-class Soul Sheet with:

- Essence;
- Attunements;
- Echoes / Soul Memories;
- Scars / Marks;
- Familiarities;
- Divine/Cosmic Relationships;
- Soul-bound Objects;
- Incarnation History.

## Essence

Possible Soul-level properties:

### Resonance Depth
Natural ability to interface with Pacha phenomena across incarnations.

### Anchoring
How strongly the Soul attaches to its current body/reality.

High anchoring may resist possession or displacement but complicate some phase/spatial effects.

### Permeability
How readily experience crosses incarnation boundaries.

High permeability can improve déjà vu/echoes while risking identity bleed or nightmares.

### Integrity
Structural condition of the Soul. This is not morality.

### Resonance Signature
Metaphysical identity recognizable by gods, Mor, soulcraft, artifacts, or advanced systems.

## Attunements

The Soul may become receptive to domains through repeated experience:

- Spatial;
- Vital;
- Soulcraft;
- Thermal;
- Otherwhere;
- Otherwhen;
- others.

Attunements are subtle biases, not inherited maxed skills.

## Soul Memories / Echoes

The Soul retains fragments rather than full memories.

Examples:

- “This vault feels familiar.”
- “You remember falling through violet light.”
- “Someone once called you Little Star.”

Strong echoes may be produced by death, love, betrayal, great mastery, divine contact, Other exposure, artifact bonds, or trauma.

## Soul Familiarity

Prior mastery may leave conceptual familiarity:

- Swordsmanship — deep;
- Smithing — moderate;
- Spatial Theory — deep.

This can improve reacquisition or recognition but does not transfer the old character's full skill value.

## Body still matters

A Soul may remember swordsmanship conceptually, but a new body has different proportions, nervous system, hands, and strength.

Motor mastery must be relearned.

A prior Kal incarnation cannot remember itself into having wings in a Veth body.

## Soul Scars and Marks

Persistent consequences may include:

- Othermarking;
- repeated resurrection;
- Soul fracture;
- divine curse/blessing;
- temporal damage.

Any consequence that crosses characters should be clearly signaled before voluntary risk.

## Soul damage and Soul death

Some threats damage body, mind, or Soul.

Soul damage can persist across incarnations.

True Soul destruction is categorically different from bodily death and should be extraordinarily rare. Actual destructive effects on player save continuity should only exist under an explicit harsh/hardcore design, if at all.

## Divine relationships

Covenants may bind to Soul identity.

The Soul Sheet can show patrons, disposition, covenant status, debts, last contact, and unknown attention.

A god may recognize a future incarnation.

## Mor recognition

Soul-sensitive Mor may recognize:

> new body, same Soul.

This enables cross-character continuity inside the fiction.

## Soul-bound artifacts

Rare objects may bind to the Soul rather than one body.

A new incarnation does not automatically receive them, but may dream about them, sense them, be recognized by them, or have unusual ways to rediscover them.

## Incarnation history

The Soul Sheet can maintain a timeline of:

- incarnation;
- major events;
- settlements founded;
- divine contact;
- Great Works;
- death/retirement;
- reincarnation.

This becomes the history of the player's world-long identity.

## Influence on character creation

The player sees current Soul state before creating a new incarnation.

Examples:

**Strong Spatial Attunement + Siann**  
May make spatial magic unusually intuitive.

**Strong Spatial Attunement + Vaskaal**  
Cannot produce direct magic, but may aid spatial science, detection, or Otherway theory.

**Othermarked + Mor**  
May unlock special recognition/dialogue.

The same Soul trait can express differently through different bodies.

## Soul progression

The Soul may develop slowly and horizontally.

Possible term: **Continuity** or **Depth**.

Growth might allow:

- retaining more echoes;
- stabilizing attunements;
- resisting Soul damage;
- clearer former-life perception;
- maintaining more bonds/covenants.

Avoid flat universal power bonuses.

## One Soul / one living incarnation

Current preference:

> **One Soul normally has one living incarnation at a time.**

However, retirement creates an unresolved design question.

Open possibilities:

- Soul remains until biological death;
- ritual release;
- time skip;
- some selectable characters are outside the main Soul lineage;
- rare Otherwhen exceptions create duplicate incarnations.

Do not lock this yet.

## Reincarnation vs resurrection

### Resurrection
Attempts to restore the same Soul to the previous/current body.

### Reincarnation
Begins a new body/life while carrying limited Soul continuity.

## Soulcraft

Advanced Soulcraft may manipulate:

- binding;
- retrieval;
- memory;
- healing;
- fragmentation;
- possession;
- reincarnation.

Potential Soul crimes include forced binding, memory stripping, fragmentation, forced reincarnation, possession, and Soul destruction.

Some cultures may regard these as worse than murder.

## Constructed Souls

If Constructed have measurable Souls, synthetic origin does not make them lesser persons. This becomes a major philosophical/religious fact.

## Gods and Soul origin

Gods may recognize, bind, heal, damage, or guide Souls without necessarily knowing where Souls ultimately originate.

## Character-selection UI

Working presentation:

**Soul / Continuity**
- Essence;
- Attunements;
- Echoes;
- Scars;
- Covenants;
- Bound Objects;
- Incarnation Timeline.

Then an **Incarnations** section for current, deceased, retired, and historical characters.

## Deletion rule

Deleting local detail about an old incarnation should not erase important Soul/world history.

## Foundational distinction

> **Character progression asks: “Who am I becoming in this life?”**  
> **Soul progression asks: “What has persisted through all of my lives?”**
