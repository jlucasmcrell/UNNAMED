The original leather, scaled, plate and mercenary sets match the distinctive source geometry, ornament and texture layouts visible in the held DevonLux adaptations. The compiler’s public liked-model list corroborates those matches. Primary author/API metadata and source thumbnails are retained in the private attribution receipts; exact metadata and held FBX/Blender hashes are recorded in `P2_ARMOUR_PROVENANCE.json`. This is an authored-source identification, not byte identity between modified meshes.

The heavy set’s helm/chest/boots/legs trace to **Mercenary by Fghdd42**. Its separate plate gauntlets trace to **Realistic armor by GreenG**, as do the repaired-proof pieces. The identically titled Mercenary Armor by marat.isliaev depicts a different garment and was rejected as a name-only lead. Other liked models are not automatically treated as sources.

Credits shipped with each accepted batch must retain both the original author/source/licence link and DevonLux’s adaptation/source link. Modifications include individual component extraction, native HumGen custom-clothing fit and skin/corrective preparation, garment layering, Godot export and rendered inventory icons. Native PBR textures remain derived from the credited source; DirectX normal green channels are converted where required. The heavy chest’s isolated loose strap is removed. No original author is replaced by the compiler credit.

Only pieces that pass both-body native movement and layering review are eligible for binding. The in-game credits and delivery receipt must carry the corresponding original/adapter entries when that batch is bound.
