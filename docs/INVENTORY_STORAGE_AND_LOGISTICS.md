# OTHERREACH — Inventory, Storage & Logistics

**Status:** Strong working direction  
**M2 impact:** None

## 1. Goal

Inventory should matter physically enough to create preparation/logistics gameplay without becoming a constant sorting chore.

## 2. Carried containers

Track some combination of:

- capacity;
- weight;
- allowed item types;
- accessibility.

Examples:

### Belt pouch
Small, fast access.

### Potion bandolier
Specialized consumable access.

### Quiver
Efficient ammunition storage.

### Backpack
General capacity, normal/slower access.

### Expedition pack
Large capacity with encumbrance/access tradeoff.

### Bag of Holding
Exceptional volume with magical rarity/cost/limitations.

## 3. Accessibility matters

An item on a belt is faster to use than an item buried in a deep pack.

Do not require excessive micromanagement to model this distinction.

## 4. Encumbrance

Weight should matter to:

- stamina;
- movement;
- climbing;
- flight;
- mount capacity;
- stealth/noise.

Avoid thresholds that instantly transform the character from normal to immobile.

## 5. Home/camp storage

Player-owned locations may have:

- chests;
- shelves;
- armories;
- warehouses;
- crafting storage;
- food storage;
- specialized secure storage.

The player should be able to view ownership/storage information without needing perfect memory.

## 6. Remote storage network

Default:

> Remote view does not automatically mean remote physical access.

Goods may move through:

- player travel;
- hirelings;
- couriers;
- caravans;
- banks;
- magical transfer;
- gate-linked infrastructure.

This preserves logistics and makes settlements valuable.

## 7. Cross-character storage

Provide a sane mechanism such as an account/legacy vault or shared bank.

Do not force players to abuse ground drops or dummy characters.

Whether shared storage is world-wide, account-wide or limited by mode needs later design.

## 8. Mail

Mail can support:

- item delivery;
- contracts;
- NPC communication;
- later multiplayer.

Physical delivery may take time depending on setting/infrastructure.

## 9. Settlement logistics

Warehouses and trade routes can improve:

- crafting supply;
- remote construction;
- merchant stock;
- NPC settlement growth.

## 10. Special storage

Possible specialized containers:

- magical isolation;
- refrigerated/alchemical storage;
- ammunition cases;
- secure evidence vaults;
- Otherwrought containment.

## 11. Design rule

> Storage convenience should be something the player can improve through infrastructure rather than a magical property of every chest.
