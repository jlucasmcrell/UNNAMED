You are an art director reviewing 2 clips from a third-person PC role-playing game, labelled A, B. Each clip is the same scripted route in a third-person game: stand, walk, run, stop, turn, sprint, stop, side views, jumps, crouch-walk (the second clip is longer: it adds jumps and a crouch). In one clip a companion follows the player. 
Review each labelled candidate independently and critically. Do not assume any candidate is better; the labels are random.
1. For EACH candidate: Would an ordinary player looking at this believe it is from a modern commercially released RPG rather than a prototype? Identify every visual reason for your answer. Give a verdict (commercial / borderline / prototype).
2. For EACH candidate: Identify every motion, transition, deformation, clipping, timing or camera issue that breaks the illusion. Give timestamps within the reel.
3. Score each candidate 1-10 on each of: animation naturalness; foot sliding; posture; locomotion; combat readability; head/neck integration; hands. Write n/a where a criterion does not apply to what is shown.
4. Rank the candidates from best to worst and say what would most improve each.
