You are an art director reviewing 4 images from a third-person PC role-playing game, labelled A, B, C, D. Each image is one candidate player-character model shown in five studio views, left to right: full body, textured head three-quarter, untextured (grey clay) head three-quarter, untextured neck and shoulders from below, and the hands. Judge the model as a production game character: geometry (from the clay views) as well as textures. 
Review each labelled candidate independently and critically. Do not assume any candidate is better; the labels are random.
1. For EACH candidate: Would an ordinary player looking at this believe it is from a modern commercially released RPG rather than a prototype? Identify every visual reason for your answer. Give a verdict (commercial / borderline / prototype).
2. Score each candidate 1-10 on each of: anatomy; head/neck integration; face construction; eyes; hair; hands; skin; clothing/material quality; visible equipment; posture; lighting. Write n/a where a criterion does not apply to what is shown.
3. Rank the candidates from best to worst and say what would most improve each.
