Review each labelled candidate independently and critically. Do not assume any candidate is better; the labels are random.

2. Score each candidate 1-10 on each of: vegetation dimensionality; visible billboard/card artifacts; tree silhouette and volume; ground material repetition; terrain/vegetation integration; lighting; scene coherence. Write n/a where a criterion does not apply to what is shown.
3. Rank the candidates from best to worst and say what would most improve each.
