Trade-offs: sword/axe higher hit ranges give slower cadence, higher requirements or cost than the Rusted Sword; knives trade reach and per-hit damage for carry weight and cadence. Stone hammer adds blunt damage but is heavy and slow. The unfit polearm is deferred. Weathered double-bit axe is cheaper and less durable than its clean counterpart; reach/cost and carry weight distinguish it from the notched axe. Carved bow is cheaper/lighter than Hunting Bow but draws slower; Halda remains the rare upgrade. Armour trades protection against carried weight, coin and repair material/skill; equal-protection heavier pieces buy durability or lower price. No armour is represented as worn-ready.
