# B-034 frontier forge camera review

Base: `aeba2f8c`, stand `(458, 741)`, player feet `(458, 7.2, 741)`.

The reported film's room orbit puts its camera at approximately
`(455.686, 8.7, 738.2422)` at frame 240. That position lies inside the south
log wall (`forge_south`: X 453..463, Z 738..738.4, ground 7.2, height 3).
The film's free Camera3D bypasses the production SpringArm3D. From that invalid
position, occlusion hides the opaque hearth and player while fire particles
remain visible. The same effect reproduces on the RC base. Temporarily disabling
occlusion restores them; that toggle is a diagnostic, not the fix.

`b034_room_gameplay.json` replaces the invalid room orbit with the existing film
driver's `gameplay` camera mode, at the normal 3.5 m chosen distance. That mode
uses the production camera and collision arm, normal field of view, and leaves
occlusion enabled. It is a drop-in shot plan for the existing private marketing
film driver, launched at the same frontier stand. The private driver itself is
not part of the RC, and this patch does not change gameplay camera code.

`room_diagnostic.gd` reproduces the old free-camera position and writes diagnostic
frames, then restores occlusion and returns to the actual game camera for the
final two frames. Its JSON records the native camera transform, FOV, arm's chosen
and collision-limited distance, and occlusion/debug flags. Set `B034_OUT` to a
private directory; pass `--script ../../tools/playtest_review/room_diagnostic.gd`
before `--`, then normal accepted AssetBase/content flags and `--stand 458,741`
after `--`. Use the shared GPU lease for at most ten minutes.

The High/noon RC views have a textured floor and a seated hearth from the normal
game camera. The old blue floor patch did not reproduce in that configuration;
it must not be described as a newly repaired art asset. The exact Ultra/sunset
comparison and Claude's verdict are recorded in the external progress ledger.

No owned art is changed or included in this public repository. Neither disabling
occlusion nor hiding the floor/hearth is an accepted final view. Claude owns the
visual verdict and disposition of any remaining non-reproducing report.
