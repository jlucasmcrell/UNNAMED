D19 catalogue-derived boss appearances
====================================

Foreman Nine:
Unit 666 - Mech by elopez7, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/unit-666-mech
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Otherreach modifications: rigid-part rig and maintenance-body action takes.
Original full-resolution PBR maps retained.

The Specification:
Red Echo environment kit from the settled owned Fab library, Fab Standard License.
Existing Spill/Stopped Gate environment attribution remains in force.
Native SM_large_module_02 source slabs (catalogue ARCH-9afe2c54784b): all7,820
source triangles retained in each of nine plates, native2K/4K PBR maps and UVs.
Otherreach modifications: uniform metric placement, rigid rule-field motion,
subtle light on the native inlay; DirectX normals adapted to OpenGL at original
resolution. No robot body, borrowed mechanical joints or generated images.

The Salt Walker:
Mech Character Model by Colorado Stark, CC0 1.0 / Public Domain.
https://opengameart.org/content/mech-character-model
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Otherreach modifications: native-rig tool-arm posture and action takes; original
anatomy, skin weights, rig and full-resolution diffuse/normal/specular maps retained.

The Thing Under the Stairs:
Octobot by piacenti, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
