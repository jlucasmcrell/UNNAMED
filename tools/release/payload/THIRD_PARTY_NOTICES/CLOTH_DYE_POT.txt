The complete 10,296-triangle source mesh and three authored 4K maps are retained.
Otherreach repairs the recovered library's flattened texture paths in a staging
copy, scales the vessel uniformly to 16 cm, moves the origin to its centre/base,
and repacks it as glTF. The icon is rendered from that same model in Godot.
No generated image art is used for this vessel, icon or wardrobe contact sheets.

Wardrobe garments remain the separately credited owned Human Generator library.
